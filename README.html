CTRL+Aid Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (version 2.0.1)

CTRL+Aid is a browser extension that helps financial aid office staff with repetitive tasks. It is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any school, software vendor or government agency.

The short version

  • CTRL+Aid has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Nothing the extension reads or stores is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Student information from the pages you have open is kept in browser memory and is cleared when the browser closes.
  • The one exception is a student you choose to save in the NSLDS + COD Printout window. That saved student is encrypted, stays on your computer, and is deleted automatically after 30 days (details below).

What the extension reads

Only when you use it, and only on the sites you allow, CTRL+Aid reads the page you have open (for example a student's name, date of birth, Social Security number, email, Portal ID, address and program on a student information page, or loan and award details on a federal financial aid site). It uses this to show you the information, copy it to your clipboard when you click, compare it between pages, fill a search box when you press a button, or build a printout PDF.

This student information is held in the browser's session memory (chrome.storage.session / page memory). It is not written to disk and is deleted when you close the browser or press Wipe student data. The Social Security number is kept in session memory only while the Floating Dock or the Printout needs it, and is never put in a log, a setting or a diagnostics copy.

Saved students (optional, only when you press Save)

In the NSLDS + COD Printout window you can save a finished student so you can reopen the printout later. Nothing is saved unless you press Save. A saved student contains the printout results and PDF, plus the student details shown in the Student details panel: name, date of birth, full Social Security number, email, Portal ID, program, start date and similar enrollment fields.

  • Where: inside the extension's own storage in your browser profile on your computer (IndexedDB). It is not sent anywhere and is not synced.
  • Encryption: each saved student is encrypted with AES-GCM (256-bit). The key is created on first save, is non-extractable, and stays in the extension. There is no password. The list of saved students shows only name, Portal ID, last four digits and dates; the full Social Security number is only inside the encrypted part.
  • How long: a saved student is deleted automatically after 30 days. Expired students are removed when you open the list and when the browser starts.
  • Deleting sooner: use Delete in the saved list, Delete all in the saved list, or Settings > Wipe student data (which removes scanned data and every saved student). Removing the extension deletes everything.
  • Limit: the encryption protects the stored files. It does not stop someone who is signed in to your computer and your browser profile from opening the extension and viewing a saved student, the same as any other page in that browser. Use a computer account and browser profile that only you can open, and lock your screen.

What the extension saves on your computer

Besides saved students, only your own settings, kept in the browser's local storage on your device: appearance (light, dark or auto), sound and time-format choices, which features are on, the position of the floating dock, the school name you type for the payment plan, school codes you enter for COD checks, and the blank payment form PDF you choose. These never leave your browser.

Permissions and why they are used

  • activeTab, scripting: read the page you opened the extension on, and show the floating dock.
  • storage: keep your settings, the temporary session memory, and the encrypted saved students described above.
  • debugger: when you press Fill or Fill & Search on the federal loan site, Chrome's debugger interface types the values with real key presses so the page accepts them. It is attached for a few seconds and detached right after. The extension also uses it to produce its printout from pages you have open and to add the student to your school system when you press the button for it. It is only attached while one of these actions runs.
  • Optional site access: the extension asks you to allow access to specific sites (your school's student information system, cod.ed.gov, nsldsfap.ed.gov and fafsa.partnerportal.ed.gov). It does nothing on a site you have not allowed. You can remove access at any time in Chrome.
  • Optional downloads: save a PDF you generate to your computer.

Third-party code

The extension bundles open-source libraries (pdf-lib and PDF.js) inside the package. It does not load or run any code from the internet.

Sharing, selling and limits on use

We do not sell or transfer user data. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and not to determine creditworthiness or for lending. The use of information received from web pages adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can turn features off in the extension's Settings, remove site access in Chrome, delete saved students or wipe all student data in the extension, remove the extension to delete everything it stored, and close the browser to clear all session memory.
